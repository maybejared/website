# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Blog readers and people assessing Jared Tucker's career and work have equal priority. The website must be usable by nontechnical visitors unfamiliar with Linux or keyboard shortcuts.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and home for blog posts. Visitors should understand Jared's background, explore his work, read articles and find contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four purpose-built workspaces:
1. Welcome: About and a workspace guide, with equal paths to work and writing.
2. Writing: posts and an article reader.
3. Experience: career history and selected work.
4. Contact: contact details and personal extras.

Each workspace opens a curated, rearrangeable layout with an obvious Restore layout button. Navigation and search must be visibly accessible without keybinds. The active workspace or layout has a persistent label.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Hyprland-inspired shell, narrow rail, tiling and desktop interactions. The user's September 2026 screenshot references guide the shell and edge-panel behaviour. Appearance should be selected as a complete bespoke design rather than separate colour and theme choices. Beige is no longer the desired default.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing portfolio content in src/content/portfolio/portfolio-content.ts and blog posts in src/content/posts/. Use actual career entries; do not invent project metrics or outcomes.

## Open Decisions

Final appearance presets and their wallpaper pairings. Exact selected-work content and personal extras. How far arrangement preferences persist beyond the current visit.
