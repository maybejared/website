Layout controls lack recovery

Title-bar dragging already works with a mouse. Resize requires the leader key; closing every window leaves an empty field. Hover also changes which window receives keys.

Proposal: explicit Focus / Restore, pointer-accessible resize and close targets, click-based activation, and a permanent Reset layout action.

Proposal: 17–18px reading type, larger identity and article headings, original prose casing, and focus indicated on the frame only.

  • Keep the interaction you already builtTiling and title-bar dragging on wide screens, restrained reflow animation, focused window edges, the launcher and optional remappable shortcuts.
  • Give every important action a visible equivalentSections are links; window operations are buttons. Search, appearance and help have names. Use a real divider for pointer resizing and buttons for focus/restore. Keep normal browser shortcuts intact.
